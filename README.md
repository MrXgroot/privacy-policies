# Privacy Policy for Stay Ledger

**Last Updated:** November 10, 2026

Stay Ledger ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an offline-first property and tenant management tool. We respect your privacy and provide this policy to explain our on-device data handling practices.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Local & Offline Storage

Stay Ledger functions entirely on your local device:
- **No Remote Application Servers:** We do not operate remote databases or external application servers that harvest, sync, or collect your business records.
- **On-Device Database:** All tenant records, room/bed allocations, financial transactions, check-ins, check-outs, and notes are stored strictly in an on-device SQLite database.
- **No Mandatory Account Registration:** Core ledger management functions do not require cloud sign-in, third-party profile linking, or social authentication.

---

## 2. Information Handled Within the App

When using Stay Ledger, you may record operational property data:
- **Tenant Information:** Names, contact phone numbers, identity notes, check-in dates, and assigned beds/rooms.
- **Financial & Transaction Records:** Rent dues, security deposits, collection records, payment channels (e.g., Cash, UPI transaction reference), and cycle adjustments.
- **Property Structure:** Floor names, room numbers, and bed configurations.

**Ownership & Privacy:** All recorded property and financial records belong solely to you. This data is never tracked, processed remotely, or sold to external third parties or advertisers.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

The App requests only standard operational permissions required for functionality:
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used strictly to trigger scheduled local, on-device alerts (such as daily rent collection reminders). No remote tracking tokens or telemetry identifiers are transmitted.
- **File Access & Sharing (Intent-Driven):** When you manually export a database backup or generate reports, the App utilizes standard Android share sheets to allow you to save or share your file. The App does not access external device storage without your direct interaction.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

- **No Third-Party Ad Trackers:** Stay Ledger contains no advertising SDKs, behavioural trackers, or analytic tracking packages.
- **Native Hand-offs (Calls / Messaging):** Direct action shortcuts (such as initiating a phone call or opening WhatsApp with a pre-filled reminder) delegate execution entirely to the respective native apps installed on your device. No conversation contents or call records are captured or stored by Stay Ledger.

---

## 5. Data Retention, Backups, and Deletion

- **Data Retention:** Data is retained inside the app sandbox on your device for as long as Stay Ledger remains installed.
- **User-Managed Backups:** Backup files (`.sqlite` or JSON exports) generated using the in-app backup utility remain under your control. You decide where they are stored, transferred, or deleted.
- **Data Erasure:** You can permanently delete all locally stored records at any time by clearing the App Storage/Data in your device's Android Settings or by uninstalling Stay Ledger.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Stay Ledger is designed for property managers and landlords. It is not intended for or directed toward children under 13, and it does not solicit or collect personal information from children.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional or regulatory changes. Any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
